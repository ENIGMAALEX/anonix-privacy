AnoniX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

AnoniX is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data Collection

We do NOT collect personal information such as name, email, or phone number.

2. Messages

Messages sent through AnoniX are:

  • Encrypted
  • Stored temporarily
  • Automatically deleted after a user-defined time

3. No Tracking

We do not track users, location, or device activity.

4. No Third Parties

We do not share data with third parties.
